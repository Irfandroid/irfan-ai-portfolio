## Context

Labels that look “clean” are not necessarily consistent. Dataset Label Analyzer is a small Streamlit application for uploading a CSV, reviewing dataset summaries and label distributions, exploring Plotly visualizations, and finding inconsistencies with RapidFuzz.

## Technical question

How can label-quality problems become visible quickly enough for someone to decide what needs deeper inspection?

## Technical decision

<TechnicalDecision
  problem="Label inspection often stops at frequency counts."
  decision="Combine summary, distribution, interactive visualization, and fuzzy matching in one exploration flow."
  reason="Data quality needs several complementary signals, not a single number."
  tradeoff="Fuzzy matching can find similar candidates, but a human still needs to review them before normalization."
/>

## Reflection

This small project reinforced my belief that a good data pipeline should make uncertainty visible. Before a model becomes smarter, its input must be open to questioning.
