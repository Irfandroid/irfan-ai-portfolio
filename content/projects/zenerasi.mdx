## Context

Zenerasi is an early exploration of a digital platform supporting users' ambitions: career preparation, professional skills, AI, and self-development. The source HTML repository contains the product identity “Zenerasi.id - Pendukung Ambisi Kamu”.

## What I explored

I read this project as practice in shaping product language and interface structure before moving into more complex systems. What is publicly visible is the product direction and front-end implementation; usage metrics, experiment results, and production status are not documented, so I do not claim them.

## Reflection

Zenerasi reminds me that systems that feel helpful begin with the right framing: who is being helped, in what moment, and what they expect after interacting.
